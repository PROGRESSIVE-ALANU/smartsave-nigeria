# 5. Mathematical methodology

Notation: $Y_t$ income, $X_t$ essential costs, $\bar N_t$ planned nonessential spending,
$F$ fees, $D$ deadline month, $E_t$ education fund, $G_t$ general savings, $r$
remainder saving rate, $\rho$ maximum nonessential cut.

$$A_t = \max(Y_t - X_t, 0), \qquad R_t = \frac{\max(F - E_t, 0)}{D - t}$$

$$A_t = N_t + C_t + S_t + U_t, \quad L_t = A_t - N_t - C_t, \quad S_t = rL_t, \quad U_t = (1-r)L_t$$

| Strategy | $N_t$ | $C_t$ |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | $\min(\bar N_t, A_t)$ | $0$ |
| S2 | $\min(\bar N_t, A_t)$ | $\min(R_t, A_t - N_t)$ |
| S3 | $\min\big(\bar N_t, \max(A_t - R_t, \min((1-\rho)\bar N_t, A_t))\big)$ | $\min(R_t, A_t - N_t)$ |

Income multiplier: $m_t = \exp(\sigma Z_t - \sigma^2/2)$ with $Z_t \sim N(0,1)$ and
$\sigma = \sqrt{\ln(1+\mathrm{CV}^2)}$, giving $E[m_t] = 1$ and coefficient of
variation CV. [Show or explain this derivation.]

Shortfall: $\max\!\big(F - E_D - G_D\,\mathbb{1}[\text{general savings usable}],\ 0\big)$.

Accounting identity verified by automated tests:
$G_0 + E_0 + \sum_t Y_t = G_T + E_T + \sum_t(\text{essentials paid} + N_t + U_t) + \text{fees paid}$.
